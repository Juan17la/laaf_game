# Audio credits

Every recorded sound here is **CC0 / public domain**: free for any use, including commercial, with no attribution
required. We credit the authors anyway. `tools/import_sfx.py` cuts and levels them into `sfx_*.wav` and
`music_*.ogg`. Only the voices, stingers, whispers, church bell, music box, bow, "!" sting and radio beep are still
synthesized by `tools/make_audio.py`.

Unpack each source into the folder named in brackets (under the sources dir you pass to `import_sfx.py`):
